Module 01

Project Overview & Real-World Problem

Understand what Aroom is, the accommodation crisis on Nigerian campuses, and how software builds trust.

1. What is Aroom?

Aroom is a verified student accommodation marketplace tailored for Nigerian university campuses, launching its pilot at the University of Lagos (UNILAG) in Akoka/Yaba.

It helps students safely find and rent off-campus self-contains, single rooms, and shared apartments, while enabling agents to manage listings and receive serious, qualified student leads.

2. What Real Problem Does Aroom Solve?

In Nigerian university towns, accommodation marketplaces typically fail not because of missing software features, but because of trust breakdowns and fraud:

  • Phantom Listings & Scams: Fake agents advertise rooms they do not manage, demand non-refundable "inspection fees" upfront, and vanish.
  • Double-Bookings: An agent rents out a room to one student, but continues collecting money or bookings from several others.
  • Hidden Move-In Costs: A student sees an advertised rent of ₦350,000/year, but discovers they actually need ₦440,000 upfront due to agency fees, agreement fees, caution deposits, and service charges.
  • Agent Frustration: Genuine agents get bombarded by non-students or inquiries from people who aren't serious.
💡 The Core Design Principle
Every feature in Aroom is filtered through one lens: Does this increase trust and remove fraud between students and agents?

3. How Software Solves These Marketplace Problems

Here is the direct mapping between student pain points and the software features in our code:

Real Problem Software Solution Built in Aroom
Fake Agents One-time phone OTP verification and ID upload. Agent trust scoring (gold, silver, bronze).
Time-Wasters Student onboarding checks university institutional email domains (e.g. @live.unilag.edu.ng).
Double-Bookings Single-tap availability toggle (available → held → taken). When inspection is booked, room is held automatically.
Phantom Rooms Crowdsourced availability reports. When a student visits and reports a room is taken, the system flags or demotes it automatically.
Hidden Upfront Fees "Total Move-In Cost" Calculator button on every property card showing rent + fees = true cost.
Slow Mobile Internet WhatsApp AI Triage Bot allows students to search and qualify rooms via lightweight chat.

4. The 3 Pillars of the Codebase

When you look at the project folders in your editor, you will see 3 distinct areas:

  1. aroom-web/ (Frontend): The React web application where users view pictures, filter prices, and click the Move-In Cost calculator.
  2. backend/ (Backend API): The Node.js and Express server that processes searches, checks student emails, and manages the WhatsApp bot.
  3. supabase/ (Database & Cloud Storage): The PostgreSQL database storing all 11 tables, user accounts, and image storage buckets.
