🍽️ How Aroom Works: The 1-Minute Restaurant Analogy

If you are new to programming, think of the entire project as a modern restaurant:

1. The Menu (Frontend aroom-web) What customers look at. Pictures of food, price tags, and buttons to place orders.
2. The Waiter (Backend backend) Takes your order to the kitchen, checks rules, calculates prices, and brings back results.
3. The Pantry (Database supabase) The secure filing cabinet storing all ingredient lists, customer accounts, and receipts.
4. Delivery Courier (WhatsApp Bot) Lets students message from their phone to browse listings and book inspections directly.
