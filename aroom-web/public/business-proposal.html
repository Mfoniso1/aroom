01. Executive Summary

Aroom Technologies is a purpose-built student housing marketplace and trust infrastructure designed to eliminate extortion, scamming, and extreme fee inflation in Nigerian tertiary institution off-campus communities. Anchored at the University of Port Harcourt (UNIPORT), Aroom addresses the acute accommodation challenges faced by over 30,000 students living outside institutional hostels.

02. The Problem: 4 Painful Realities Every UNIPORT Student Knows

Every semester, students and their parents face systematic exploitation by unregulated street agents stationed around Choba Junction, UPTH Gate, and Delta Park:

Trap #1
The "Inspection Fee" Trap
Roadside agents demand ₦5,000 to ₦10,000 per trip merely to view a room. After 3 to 4 fruitless excursions under the hot sun or rain, students routinely burn ₦20,000–₦35,000 with no room secured.
Trap #2
Bait-and-Switch Tactics
Agents post attractive photos of neat self-contains to lure students to Choba, only to announce upon arrival: "Ah, somebody just paid 20 minutes ago! Let me take you to another one far inside Aluu."
Trap #3
Shocking Hidden Move-In Fees
A lodge advertised as ₦220,000/year suddenly balloons into ₦360,000+ at payment time due to unannounced 20–25% agency fees, arbitrary legal agreement charges, and caution fees.
Trap #4
Rogue Caretakers & Rental Scams
Freshers (Year 1 students) and parents transferring rent from distant states frequently pay impostors claiming to be "landlords," only to discover the lodge was rented to someone else or does not exist.

03. The Aroom Solution: 4 Integrated Pillars

Pillar 1
₦0 Free Physical Inspections
Students book accompanied physical inspections with accredited agents without paying a single kobo. Inspection fees are strictly prohibited across the platform.
Pillar 2
Team Lead Ground Verification
Before any lodge or agent is certified, the local Aroom Team Lead physically inspects the compound: verifying treated borehole water, prepaid electricity meters, and security gating.
Pillar 3
Move-In Cost Calculator
Every listing displays the exact itemized total upfront: Annual Rent + Caution + Legal + Utility + capped 15% agency commission (or 0% Agency Fee for Direct Landlords).
Pillar 4
WhatsApp AI Concierge
Students interact directly on WhatsApp to query available rooms by budget, landmark (e.g. "near Choba Gate under ₦280k"), view video walkthroughs, and schedule verified inspections.

04. Monetization & Business Model

Stream 1
Agent Commission Split
Agents charge a capped 15% agency fee on completed leases. Aroom takes a 25% platform share (avg ₦9,750 per closed lease on a ₦260,000 room) for qualified lead generation and automated scheduling.
Stream 2
Landlord Priority Placement
Multi-unit student lodges (10–30 rooms) pay ₦15,000 – ₦25,000 per semester for featured badge placement on Web and priority recommendation on the WhatsApp AI Concierge.
Stream 3
Standardized Tenancy Packs
Digital tenancy agreements, formal rent receipt generation, and room inventory condition checklists supplied at a flat fee of ₦3,000 per tenancy pack.
