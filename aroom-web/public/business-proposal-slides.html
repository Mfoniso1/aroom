Revenue Model

How Aroom Generates High-Margin Revenue

A sustainable, multi-sided marketplace monetizing volume through closed leases and premium placement.

Revenue Stream 1
Agent Marketplace Split

Aroom caps verified agent commission at 15% (avoiding 25% street extortion) and retains a 25% platform share (~₦9,375 per lease) on closed rentals for providing pre-qualified leads.

Revenue Stream 2
Landlord Priority Boost

Direct landlords listing 15–20 units can purchase Featured Lodge Placement (₦15,000–₦25,000/semester) to guarantee top placement on the web directory and priority recommendation on WhatsApp.

Revenue Stream 3
Standard Tenancy Packs

Standardized digital tenancy agreements, formal rent receipt generation, and room condition inspection checklists at a flat fee of ₦3,000 per lease package.

Ancillary Student Services: Partnerships with campus micro-logistics movers for student luggage relocation (10% referral cut) and verified prepaid electricity token integration.
