Supply Architecture
The Curated Cohort

The Supply Engine: Why We Are NOT an Open Marketplace

Open platforms get infested by scammers. Aroom establishes an invite-only team of vetted, credible partners.

The 15–20 Partner Quota Model
15–20 Partners × 15–20 Listings = 225–300 Verified Rooms/Month

• Manageable Individual Quota: Each partner lists 15 properties/month (approx. 1 property every 2 days).
• Concentrated Quality: Covers 100% of major UNIPORT student clusters: Choba Gate, Alakahia (UPTH), Delta Park, Back of Chem, and Ozuoba.
• Total Accountability: If an agent demands an inspection fee, their verified badge is permanently revoked.

Why Open Platforms Fail in Nigeria
Open Marketplaces = Guaranteed Fraud & Decay

• Open classifieds (Jiji, Facebook) have no physical verification—anyone can post stolen photos of someone else's house.
• Students lose money to fake online caretakers demanding booking deposits.
• Aroom's Moat: By keeping supply invite-only and physically verified by our Team Lead, we guarantee 100% genuine rooms.
